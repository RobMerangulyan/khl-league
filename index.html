Budget. Every manager has 20 coins and must own exactly 4 clubs. Club prices run from 1 to 10 coins.

When points count. By default your clubs score for every game they play after you save your team. The organiser can switch the league to count each club's points from the start of the season.

Transfers. You can sell and buy clubs at any time. Points your old clubs earned for you stay on your total. A new club starts scoring from the moment you save.

Results. Results come from the official KHL site. During game hours they refresh about every minute.

Private leagues. On the League table, tap + New / join to create a league for your friends and share its code or invite link. A league table shows only its members. You can be in several leagues.

App. Add the site to your home screen to use it like an app: on Android or a computer, tap Install when the site offers it; on iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
